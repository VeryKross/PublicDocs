The short version

  • Your saved rides stay on your iPhone unless you share them or your phone backs them up. The app works out routes on your phone using downloaded state maps. You do not need a Curvy Cruiser account.
  • No ads or tracking. Curvy Cruiser does not include tools that track your use of the app for advertising or send us usage reports.
  • Some features use the internet. Apple handles map pictures and place searches. A map download goes to the download provider. When you share a ride, the app you choose handles the file.

This notice covers the invitation-only test version. Ride sharing is still being tested. See how to send a ride with Outlook and why Apple Mail does not work yet.

What is saved on your iPhone

Curvy Cruiser saves your Home location, ride settings, saved rides, and downloaded maps on your iPhone. A saved ride can include the full route, exact start and finish, stops, settings, warnings, and labels.

The app uses your location to plan and follow rides. While you are navigating, it can keep using your location when another app is open or you use CarPlay. It does not keep a background history of your trips. You can change location permission in iPhone Settings.

Your iPhone may include app information in a backup. What is backed up and restored depends on your phone's settings and Apple's policies. Saving something on your phone does not guarantee that it will never be included in a backup.

When maps and searches use the internet

State maps download directly from Geofabrik, using road information from OpenStreetMap. Your iPhone then uses those downloads to work out routes.

Geofabrik receives the download you ask for and your IP address, the internet address used for your connection. It may keep connection records under its own policies. See Geofabrik's privacy information and the OpenStreetMap credits and license.

Apple provides the map pictures and searches for addresses and places. When you search for a destination, that search is sent to Apple. These features may need internet access even after you download state maps. See Apple's privacy information.

Apple also provides TestFlight, which may send Apple feedback and information about crashes or other problems under its settings and terms. Apple and other providers may keep connection or problem reports even though Curvy Cruiser has no separate system for collecting usage reports.

A shared ride can reveal private places

If your test version includes sharing, you can send a ride file ending in .curvyride. Your friend chooses whether to add it to the app and gets a separate saved copy. Curvy Cruiser does not require an account or iCloud to do this.

The file contains the full route, exact start and finish, stops, ride settings, warnings, and labels. It can reveal your home, a meeting place, or another private location. If a ride starts at Home, your home location is still in the file. Your friend's copy uses their own saved Home to work out the route, but that does not hide your location in the file you sent.

Review a ride before sending it. Your friend can keep or forward it. Changing or deleting your ride later does not change their copy, and you cannot take the file back.

The email, messaging, or file-saving service you choose handles the ride under its own privacy rules and may require an account. The protection of the file depends on that service; Curvy Cruiser does not guarantee that only you and your friend can read it.

Deleting rides and other saved information

Your saved rides and maps stay on your phone until you remove them. Use the app's available controls to delete rides or maps, or change your Home and ride settings. Removing the app deletes its saved information from your iPhone, but not phone backups or ride files saved elsewhere.

There is no Curvy Cruiser account to close. Manage phone backups in iPhone Settings, and delete ride files from any email or file-saving apps where you kept them.

Your friends control their own copies. Email and file-saving services may also keep records or backups under their own rules. Deleting a ride from your phone does not delete those copies. We cannot promise when another person or service will remove them.

These pages and support messages

These pages do not use cookies, advertising, or tools that track what you read. There is no sign-in or form for sending files. GitHub Pages runs this website. GitHub keeps visitors' IP addresses for security and may use basic connection information to run the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you ask for help, we use your contact details and message to answer you. Your email or messaging service handles those messages under its own privacy rules. Do not send passwords, invitation links, or private ride files.

Your choices and questions

You choose whether to share a ride. You can change location permission in iPhone Settings, delete saved rides, and manage your phone backups and shared files separately.

For a privacy question, a correction, or a request about information you sent when asking for help, contact the person who invited you. We cannot remove copies held by your friends or other services.

This notice describes the test version. If how Curvy Cruiser handles information changes, we will update this page and its date.
