Use Curvy Cruiser on your iPhone to plan motorcycle rides in the United States. Here's how to get ready, share a ride, and get help.

Friends-and-family test version, by invitation. Install through Apple's TestFlight app. Requires iOS 17 or later. Features may differ between test versions.

You do not need a Curvy Cruiser account to plan or save rides.

Install the test version

Follow the TestFlight invitation from the person who invited you. Apple must approve the test version, and your testing group must have access, before you can install it.

If TestFlight says "No Builds Available," a version is not ready for you yet. Wait for a notification or contact the person who invited you. You do not need to delete saved rides to fix this message.

This is an early test version. Set it up and try it only while parked safely. Do not use your iPhone's controls while riding.

Download before you head out

Download the state maps you need over Wi-Fi before leaving. Leave enough free space on your iPhone and allow time for the maps to finish getting ready.

Once those downloads are ready, your iPhone can work out routes without an internet connection. The map pictures you see and searches for addresses or places use Apple services and may still need internet access.

A route will not load, or a road is missing

While parked, check that maps for every state on your ride have finished downloading. Check your iPhone's free storage, then download or update the maps over Wi-Fi and try again. Even an updated map may have missing roads or out-of-date information.

Our road information comes from OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License. Geofabrik provides the state downloads.

Check your route before you ride

Always follow road signs, closures, access restrictions, and your own judgment. A suggested route is not a guarantee that a road is open or safe for your motorcycle. Road surfaces and speed limits shown in the app may be missing or out of date.

The app normally avoids unpaved roads. It may still use a short unpaved stretch, or a road whose surface is unknown, to reach your start, destination, or a stop. Read the route warnings before setting out.

Ride times do not allow for traffic, stops, or weather. The app does not provide weather forecasts or spoken directions. If you leave the planned route, it will not automatically find you a new one. Stop somewhere safe before checking or changing your route.

Your position is not updating

While parked, check that Curvy Cruiser has permission to use your location in iPhone Settings. Allow time for your phone to find your position. During navigation, the app can keep using your location while another app is open or you use CarPlay. It does not keep a background history of your trips.

Using CarPlay

CarPlay is still being tested with this version and actual vehicles. Check that it works with your setup while parked, before relying on it for a ride.

Send a ride to a friend

If your test version includes ride sharing, you can send a ride as a file ending in .curvyride. Sending it through Outlook and opening it on the recipient's iPhone have been tested successfully.

Use Outlook rather than Apple Mail for now. Apple Mail currently sends your message without the ride file. Check that the file is attached before sending. Other sharing apps are still being checked and may not work.

Your friend chooses whether to add the ride to Curvy Cruiser. They get their own saved copy. Later changes to your ride do not change theirs, and you cannot take back a file once you have sent it.

A shared ride can reveal your home. The file includes the route, start, finish, stops, ride settings, warnings, and labels. If it starts at Home, your home location is still included, even though your friend's copy uses their own saved Home to work out their route. Review the ride before sharing it.

Your friend can save or forward the file. Curvy Cruiser does not require an account or iCloud to share a ride, but the app you choose to send or save it may have its own requirements. Read what sharing means for your privacy.

Manage your saved rides

Your Home location, ride settings, saved rides, and downloaded maps are kept on your iPhone. Your phone may include app information in its backups, depending on your settings.

Use the app's available controls to delete rides or downloaded maps, or change your Home and ride settings. Removing the app deletes its information from your iPhone. It does not delete phone backups or ride files you or your friends have saved elsewhere. There is no Curvy Cruiser account to close.

Contact support

For help installing the app, planning a ride, or understanding your privacy, contact the person who invited you using the details in your invitation.

Tell them which app and iPhone software versions you are using, which state maps you downloaded, and what went wrong. If you had a CarPlay problem, explain what happened when you tried it while parked.

Do not send passwords, invitation links, or private ride files. Hide your home and other private places in any screenshots you send.
